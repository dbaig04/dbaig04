## Danial Baig

I build low-latency trading systems in Rust, focused on prediction markets and crypto. Most of my work is about finding pricing inefficiencies and executing on them faster than the rest of the market.

### Current projects

- **OnChainArbitrageSystem**: detects and executes arbitrage opportunities across chains (Rust)
- **CryptoLiquidationEngine**: monitors on-chain positions and acts on liquidation events (TypeScript)
- **Polymarket-Arbitrage**: captures mispricings across Polymarket markets (Rust)

Source code is private. Open to connecting with anyone working on trading infrastructure or market microstructure.
