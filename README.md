## Danial Baig

I build low-latency trading systems in Rust, mostly around prediction markets and crypto.

### Current projects

- **Polymarket-Arbitrage** (Rust): market making bot for Polymarket sports markets. It quotes both sides pre-game and live, keeps spreads tight to collect liquidity rewards, and requotes fast when prices move mid-game. It also has position limits so one bad game doesn't wreck the book.
- **OnChainArbitrageSystem** (Rust): finds and executes arbitrage across chains
- **CryptoLiquidationEngine-01** (TypeScript): tracks on-chain positions and acts on liquidations

Code's private, but always down to talk with people building trading infrastructure
